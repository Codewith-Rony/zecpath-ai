JOB DESCRIPTION: Senior HR Business Partner
Job ID: JD04
Level: Senior / typically 8+ years
Department: Human Resources

ROLE SUMMARY
We are seeking a Senior HR Business Partner to advise business leaders and align people strategy with organizational priorities.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Partner with senior leaders on workforce planning and organizational effectiveness.
- Advise on employee relations, performance management and people policies.
- Lead or influence talent, succession and engagement programs.
- Use HR metrics and analytics to identify workforce trends.
- Coach managers and support organizational change.
- Ensure HR practices are consistent with applicable policies and employment requirements.

REQUIRED CRITERIA
- Substantial HR experience, typically 8 or more years.
- Demonstrated HR business partnering or equivalent strategic HR advisory experience.
- Experience advising managers or business leaders on employee relations and performance matters.
- Workforce planning, talent management or organizational development experience.
- Strong communication, stakeholder management and coaching skills.
- Evidence of using HR data or metrics to inform decisions.

PREFERRED CRITERIA
- HR analytics and reporting tools.
- HR information systems such as Workday or similar.
- Experience supporting multiple departments, locations or a large employee population.
- Experience leading HR teams or major people programs.

EXPERIENCE EXPECTATION
This is a senior strategic role. Candidates should show evidence of advising leaders and influencing people decisions, not only processing HR administration.

MANUAL REVIEW NOTES
Assess strategic partnership, scope, leadership influence, employee relations and workforce planning. A senior HR title without supporting responsibilities is not sufficient evidence.
