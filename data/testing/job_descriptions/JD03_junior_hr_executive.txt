JOB DESCRIPTION: Junior HR Executive
Job ID: JD03
Level: Entry-level / 0–2 years
Department: Human Resources

ROLE SUMMARY
We are seeking a Junior HR Executive to support recruitment coordination, onboarding and day-to-day HR administration.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Coordinate interview schedules and communicate with candidates.
- Maintain candidate trackers and recruitment documentation.
- Support onboarding activities and employee record administration.
- Assist with campus hiring and HR events.
- Prepare basic reports using spreadsheets.
- Handle confidential information responsibly.

REQUIRED CRITERIA
- Understanding of basic HR or recruitment processes.
- Strong written and verbal communication.
- Organization, attention to detail and follow-through.
- Basic spreadsheet and document-management skills.
- Relevant education or practical experience in HR, business administration or a related area.
- Evidence of coordination, administration or people-support work through projects, internships or employment.

PREFERRED CRITERIA
- Internship or project experience in recruitment or HR operations.
- Excel or Google Sheets.
- Exposure to an applicant tracking system or HR information system.
- Familiarity with onboarding documentation.

EXPERIENCE EXPECTATION
Fresh graduates and candidates with up to 2 years of relevant experience are encouraged. A formal HR job title is not mandatory if the candidate demonstrates relevant coordination and administration skills.

MANUAL REVIEW NOTES
Look for evidence of scheduling, candidate communication, records, onboarding and spreadsheet use. Do not assume suitability based on degree title alone.
