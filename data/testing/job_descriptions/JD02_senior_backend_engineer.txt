JOB DESCRIPTION: Senior Backend Engineer
Job ID: JD02
Level: Senior / typically 6+ years
Department: Engineering

ROLE SUMMARY
We are seeking a Senior Backend Engineer to design, deliver and operate reliable backend services and mentor engineers.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design and implement scalable backend services and REST APIs.
- Make sound architecture and data-modeling decisions.
- Improve service performance, reliability and observability.
- Work with databases, caching and asynchronous or event-driven systems.
- Review code, mentor engineers and coordinate technical delivery.
- Support production deployments and incident resolution.

REQUIRED CRITERIA
- Strong professional backend engineering experience, typically 6 or more years.
- Significant hands-on experience with Python or another production backend language.
- Experience designing and maintaining APIs or distributed services.
- Strong SQL and relational database skills.
- Evidence of production ownership, system design or performance/reliability improvements.
- Experience collaborating through code review and technical mentoring.

PREFERRED CRITERIA
- Python frameworks such as Django, FastAPI or Flask.
- PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka or similar technologies.
- Docker, Kubernetes and cloud platforms such as AWS.
- CI/CD, monitoring and infrastructure automation.

EXPERIENCE EXPECTATION
This is a senior individual-contributor role. Relevant depth and demonstrated ownership matter more than an exact job title. Candidates with fewer years may be considered only where equivalent senior-level responsibility is clearly evidenced.

MANUAL REVIEW NOTES
Assess depth, scale, ownership, design decisions and mentoring. Do not treat a list of tools without work examples as sufficient evidence of senior capability.
