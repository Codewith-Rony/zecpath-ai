Day 17 Manual Review Rubric — Zecpath AI

Use this rubric for each candidate–job description pair BEFORE viewing the ATS score or recommendation.

Decision labels:
- suitable: meets the defined minimum requirements with evidence.
- not_suitable: does not meet one or more essential requirements.
- borderline: evidence is incomplete or mixed; requires human review.

Score each dimension from 0 to 2:
0 = no evidence / does not meet
1 = partial or transferable evidence
2 = clear evidence

Dimensions:
1. Essential skills or functional capabilities
2. Relevant experience and level
3. Role-specific responsibilities / practical evidence
4. Education or equivalent practical preparation (where the JD requires it)

Suggested interpretation (guidance, not an automatic hiring rule):
- 6–8, with no critical requirement missing: suitable
- 0–3 or a critical requirement clearly missing: not_suitable
- 4–5, ambiguous evidence, or conflicting evidence: borderline

Important:
- Apply the JD's experience expectations. A fresher JD must not require senior experience.
- Do not use name, age, gender, location, photograph, or other non-job-related personal attributes.
- Record a short evidence note for every decision.
- Make the manual decision before checking ATS output where practical.
- Do not use the resume's stated target title as proof of suitability.
- Keep borderline cases separate from binary precision/recall calculations unless your evaluation protocol explicitly defines how they are handled.
